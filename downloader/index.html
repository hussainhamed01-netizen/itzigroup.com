      FAQ

      Good to know

      Is the TikTok video really watermark-free?

      Yes. TikTok stores a clean version of every video for playback. We download that one. If a platform only offers a watermarked copy, the option is clearly labelled.

      Can I download stories and private posts?

      Public stories work on platforms that allow it, such as Snapchat. Instagram stories and anything private need a signed-in account on the server, which the site owner sets up. We will never ask for your password.

      Which sites are supported?

      TikTok, Instagram, YouTube (including Shorts), X / Twitter, Facebook, Snapchat, Threads, Pinterest, Reddit, Bluesky, Tumblr, Vimeo, Twitch, SoundCloud, Dailymotion, Bilibili and over a thousand others. If a link fails, paste the link to the post itself rather than a profile page.

      What do you keep about me?

      Your link is used only to fetch the media. Results and prepared files are deleted from the server automatically within about 30 minutes. Your recent links are stored only in this browser, and you can clear them at any time.

      Am I allowed to download this?

      Only download content you own, have permission to use, or that is licensed for reuse. Respect creators and each platform's terms.
