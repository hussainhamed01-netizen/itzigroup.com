Introducing .itzinote & ItziPDF Sync

Two flagship apps engineered to work together privately on your device—zero cloud hops.

  1. 1

    Study Any PDF from ItziPDF

    Tap Study in ItziNotes inside ItziPDF's Reader or Result Sheet. ItziNotes imports every slide into a multi-page study notebook where you can highlight, sketch, insert blank study sheets between slides, and record synced lecture audio.

  2. 2

    Open, Portable .itzinote Container

    Every notebook is a clean ZIP file bundling manifest.json, document.json, compact binary .inkbin vector strokes, deduplicated media & custom fonts, and a cached vector export.pdf—so your work is never locked in.

  3. 3

    Send Straight to ItziPDF Tools

    Tap the PDF icon in ItziNotes to export a crisp vector PDF—or send the native .itzinote file—straight into ItziPDF 2.0 to compress, merge, watermark, password-protect, or sign in one tap.

FAQ

Common questions

Does ItziNotes require an account or upload my notebooks to the cloud?

Never. ItziNotes 1.0 runs 100% locally on your phone or tablet—it has no accounts, no analytics, and does not even declare the Android INTERNET permission. You control backups anytime via .itzibackup and .itzinote exports.

How does ItziNotes work with Samsung S Pen and Android tablets?

ItziNotes automatically adapts to tablets with a persistent Navigation Rail and multi-column shelves, enables 120Hz/144Hz display refresh rates, and supports S Pen hover cursors, pressure & tilt curves, customizable side-button shortcuts, inverted erasers, and full Stylus-Only palm rejection.

Can I open .itzinote files in ItziPDF?

Yes! ItziPDF natively recognizes .itzinote files shared or opened from ItziNotes, extracts the crisp embedded vector PDF on-device, and lets you read, compress, merge, sign, or protect it immediately.
