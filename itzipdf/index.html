What Is ItziPDF?

ItziPDF is a complete PDF studio for Android created by ItziGroup. It helps students, professionals, businesses and everyday users read, convert, organize, edit, sign and protect documents without complicated desktop software, without accounts and without uploading anything.

Every tool runs on your device. Version 2.0 is our biggest update yet: a new design, a reader built for very large files, a local Word to PDF engine and more than 30 tools.

Word to PDF, Offline

Convert Word documents (.docx and .doc) to PDF directly on your phone with our own conversion engine. Headings, lists, tables, images, headers, footers, page numbers and footnotes are kept, and Arabic and mixed Arabic-English text is shaped and ordered correctly. Convert one file or many at once.

What You Can Do

  • Read: open PDFs with 1,000+ pages smoothly, zoom sharply, search, use bookmarks and the table of contents, night and sepia modes, right-to-left page progression and Read aloud
  • Organize: Page Studio to drag, rotate, duplicate, delete and insert pages; extract or remove pages, remove blank pages, crop margins and add blank pages
  • Merge and split: merge any number of PDFs and images with page selection or duplex interleaving; split by ranges, every N pages, single pages, size, bookmarks or by tapping between pages
  • Compress: Light, Recommended, Strong and Extreme presets with before-and-after sizes
  • Create and convert: Word to PDF, scanner with edge detection, images to PDF, text to PDF, notebook and planner paper; export pages as images, extract text or embedded images
  • Edit and sign: watermarks, page numbers, headers and footers with Hijri dates and Bates numbering, bilingual stamps, saved signatures and annotations, all with live previews
  • Protect and clean: AES-256 passwords with permissions, unlock, metadata editing and cleanup, flatten, repair, PDF/A, grayscale, permanent redaction and side-by-side comparison
  • Batch and recipes: run a tool on many files or save chains such as Word to PDF → Compress → Page numbers → Protect
  • Open, rename, share, save or send every result straight into another tool

Designed for Privacy

Your documents are processed on your device and are never uploaded to ItziGroup. ItziPDF has no account and runs no analytics. Some features use services on your phone, such as Google's on-device document scanner, your text-to-speech voice or Android's print system; the ItziPDF Privacy Policy explains exactly what they involve.

Completed files are kept in the app's private Library until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Reading positions, saved signatures and tool settings stay on your device.

Made for English and Arabic

ItziPDF is fully bilingual with native right-to-left layouts, Arabic-Indic digits, Hijri dates, Arabic-friendly file names, correct Arabic in generated PDFs and Arabic search that understands different letter forms.

Make It Yours

Choose the Itzi orange theme or another palette, light, dark or pure-black mode, Material You colors, text size, reduced motion, quick actions and tool layout.

Why We Created ItziPDF

PDF tools are often scattered across websites and apps, many of them paywalled or requiring uploads to unknown servers. We created ItziPDF to give everyone a fast, private and genuinely free PDF workspace on their phone, in English and Arabic.

Good to Know

Large documents are limited only by your phone's storage, memory and speed; every tool shows progress and can be cancelled. A few options, such as Extreme compression and Grayscale, save pages as images and say so before you run them. Word conversion covers most documents faithfully; content like charts, SmartArt or text wrapped around floating pictures may look different, and the app tells you when it finds it. Always review important documents before you share or submit them. See the ItziPDF Terms of Use.
