How It Works

Pick, process, and chain tools effortlessly

Never lose your originals—every tool creates a clean output ready to share, save, or send straight into the next step.

  1. 1

    Open, share, or scan

    Choose files from your device or ItziPDF Files, open PDFs and Word documents directly from other apps, scan paper pages, or start from launcher shortcuts.

  2. 2

    Process privately on-device

    Pick your preset or customize options with live previews. Every operation runs locally with real progress reporting and instant cancel support.

  3. 3

    Save, share, or chain

    Open in the reader, rename, share, copy to your chosen folder, or tap Continue With to chain tools—or save reusable Batch recipes like Word → Compress → Page Numbers → Protect.

Designed for privacy—and transparent about every detail

Your documents are processed directly on your phone and are never uploaded to ItziGroup. ItziPDF requires no account and runs no analytics or crash-reporting service.

  • Completed outputs stay in ItziPDF's private Library until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app. You can also copy outputs to any folder you choose or share them.
  • Reading positions, bookmarks, saved signatures and Batch recipes stay on your device; PDF passwords are never saved.
  • No camera permission and no broad "All files access": scanning uses Google's on-device ML Kit scanner (via Google Play services) or your phone's own camera app.
  • Optional features like Read aloud and Printing use your device's installed text-to-speech engine and Android's print system.

FAQ

Common questions

Does ItziPDF upload my PDFs or Word documents?

No. Every document tool in ItziPDF 2.0—including offline Word to PDF, compression, signing, redaction, and encryption—runs locally on your Android device. ItziGroup never receives your files, file names, passwords, signatures, or reading history.

Is ItziPDF really free, with no account required?

Yes. ItziPDF 2.0 has no accounts, no subscriptions, no in-app purchases, and no watermarks added to your files. All 34 tools are unlocked right after you install the app.

Are there strict page or file size limits in 2.0?

Version 2.0 removed the old 500-page, 250 MB, and batch count caps. You can read PDFs with 1,000+ pages smoothly, merge any number of PDFs and images, and process large files limited only by your phone's storage, memory and speed. Text to PDF accepts up to 2,000,000 characters (or imported text files up to 8 MB), and every tool shows progress and can be cancelled.

How well does offline Word to PDF work with Arabic documents?

Our built-in engine converts .docx, .docm, .dotx and .doc files locally with bundled Arabic and metric-compatible Latin fonts. Headings, lists, tables, images, headers, footers, footnotes, and mixed Arabic–English bidirectional text are shaped and ordered cleanly. If a file contains elements that may look different—such as charts, SmartArt, or text wrapped around floating pictures—the app notifies you.

Where are my created PDFs saved?

Every output is saved in ItziPDF's private Library (visible under the Files tab) without ever overwriting your original file. You can also save an independent copy to any folder, configure automatic copying to a custom folder in Settings → Files & export, or share directly to another app. Remember to back up or export important Library files before uninstalling the app.

Does Compress PDF keep my text selectable?

Yes for Light, Recommended, and Strong presets—text stays selectable and searchable, and links, bookmarks, and form fields are kept. Extreme compression (and Grayscale) save pages as images for maximum reduction or monochrome output, and the app warns you clearly before running them.
