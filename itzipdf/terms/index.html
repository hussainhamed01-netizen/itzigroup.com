By using ItziPDF you agree to these terms. Use the app only with files you own or have the legal right to view, change and share.

Your responsibility

Keep a backup of important documents. The ItziPDF Library lives inside the app's storage and is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall it; use Save copy, a custom output folder, or Share when you need an independent copy. ItziPDF never overwrites the file you started from, but you are responsible for reviewing every result before you print, send, submit or rely on it.

Conversions and document fidelity

Word to PDF, Text to PDF and other conversions run entirely on your device with bundled, metric-compatible fonts. Most documents convert faithfully, but some content can look different from the original application — for example text that flows around floating pictures, charts, SmartArt, EMF/WMF drawings, rare fonts, or older .doc files. The app tells you when it detects such content. Check converted documents before using them for official purposes.

Some options (for example Extreme compression, Grayscale or certain repairs) save pages as images; the app says so before and after, and text in those pages may no longer be selectable or searchable.

Signatures, stamps and redaction

Signatures and stamps added with ItziPDF are visual marks placed on the page. They are not certificate-based digital signatures and do not by themselves prove identity or intent; whether a document signed this way is accepted is up to you and the recipient, under the law that applies to you.

Redaction permanently removes the selected text and covers the area. ItziPDF verifies the result, but you should review redacted files before sharing them. Redaction cannot be undone in the output file.

Allowed use

Do not use ItziPDF to create, edit or share illegal, fraudulent, harmful or rights-infringing content, or to forge signatures or documents. Use Protect and Unlock only for files you own or are authorized to process, and only with passwords you are entitled to use. Respect the copyright and licences of the documents you work with.

Service limits

The tools are provided as they are and depend on your device's storage, memory and performance. Very large files take longer and may exceed what a device can handle; the app shows progress and lets you cancel. This release has no cloud processing and no accounts.

Third-party services

Some features rely on services on your device: Google Play services (for the document scanner and in-app reviews), your text-to-speech engine (Read aloud) and Android's print system. Their providers' terms apply to them. Fonts and open-source components bundled with ItziPDF are used under their licences, listed in Settings → About → Open-source licences.

Ads are third-party content and not an endorsement by Itzi Group. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases in this release; if paid digital features are ever offered through Google Play, Google Play's payment policies will apply.

No warranty and liability

We work hard to make ItziPDF reliable, but we do not guarantee that every file will open or convert, or that every result will be accepted by every system or third party. To the extent permitted by law, Itzi Group is not liable for loss caused by unsupported files, device limitations or misuse. Nothing in these terms limits consumer rights or liability that cannot legally be excluded.
