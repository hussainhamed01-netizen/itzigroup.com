Summary

ItziPDF is a free, local-first PDF toolkit. It does not require an account. Every document tool in this release — including Word to PDF conversion, scanning, signing, redaction and compression — runs on your device. Itzi Group does not receive your documents, their text, your passwords, signatures, images, file names, Library catalog or reading history, and does not sell or share them. Advertising is supported only in builds where it is enabled (see "Advertising and privacy choices").

Files you choose

ItziPDF uses only the files you pick or send to it: PDFs, Word documents (.docx, .docm, .dotx, .doc), images, text and Markdown files, and camera scans. You can choose them with Android's pickers, open them from another app ("Open with"), share them to ItziPDF, or start from a home-screen shortcut. A file received from another app may be copied into ItziPDF's private cache so it can be read or processed.

Results are saved in the app's private Library with local details such as file name, tool used, page count, size and creation time. You can save another copy to a folder you choose, to your gallery (for exported images), or share it with another app. A chosen folder may be on the device, an SD card or a cloud storage provider; that provider controls what happens to the copy you deliberately save there.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work and remember your choices, ItziPDF keeps these records in its private storage. None of them is uploaded to Itzi Group:

  • Preferences: language, appearance and theme, number style, tool organization, quick actions, output folder and naming pattern, reader defaults.
  • Usage helpers: favorite tools, recently used tools and how often you use them, used only to order tools for you.
  • Reading state: for up to 200 recent documents, the last page, named bookmarks, view mode, rotation and reading appearance. A compact local fingerprint (from the file size and small samples of its beginning and end) recognizes a file you open again; the records contain neither those samples nor the document content. A list of up to 30 recently opened documents (name, location, page count, last page) powers "Continue reading".
  • Tool settings: your last-used options per tool and any Batch recipes you save. Passwords are never saved.
  • Saved signatures and initials: the images you draw, type or import in Sign PDF stay in private app storage until you delete them.
  • Thumbnails: small page previews in the app cache to make browsing fast. You can clear them in Settings → Storage; Android may also clear the cache.
  • Error log: if the app hits an unexpected error, a short technical note (time and error details, no document content) is kept in a small local file. It is never sent automatically; you may choose to include it when you contact support.

Device services used by some features

  • Document scanner: Scan to PDF uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, provided through Google Play services when your device supports it. Pages are captured and processed on your device and returned to ItziPDF; the images are not sent to Itzi Group. Google states that ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information — for example device model and Android version, app package name and version, performance metrics and error codes — encrypted in transit, to operate and improve the service. On devices without it, ItziPDF uses your camera with on-device cropping and filters instead. You can also create PDFs from your own photos with Images to PDF.
  • Read aloud: the reader can speak a page using the text-to-speech engine installed on your device. The page text is passed to that engine. Most engines work offline; if you selected an engine that uses an online service, its provider's terms and privacy policy apply.
  • Printing: when you print, Android's print system sends the document to the printer or print service you choose.
  • Google Play in-app review: if you choose to rate the app, Google Play shows the review prompt. ItziPDF does not receive your review text, rating or Google account details; Itzi Group may see published reviews in Google Play Console.

Permissions

  • Camera: requested only when you use the in-app camera for Scan to PDF on devices without the Google document scanner. The Google scanner itself does not need ItziPDF to hold the camera permission.
  • Files and folders: Android's pickers give access only to the items you select. Choosing an output folder grants ongoing access so future copies can be saved there; when you change the folder or return to automatic Library storage, the app stops using the previous folder and asks Android to release the old grant (a storage provider may not revoke access immediately).
  • Internet and network state: used only for advertising and privacy messages in builds where ads are enabled, and by Google Play services (for example to install the scanner module). Document processing never needs the internet.

ItziPDF does not request contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone, location, or broad "All files access".

Advertising and privacy choices

Builds with advertising enabled use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Banners are limited to the Home and Files screens and are never shown inside tools or the reader. Enabled builds request non-personalized ads, and reading, editing and processing never require watching an ad. In builds where advertising is disabled, the advertising SDK is not initialized and no ad requests are made.

When ads are enabled, Google and its advertising partners may collect and share IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them; advertising, app-set or other device identifiers where applicable; device and app information; app and ad interactions; and diagnostic or performance information, for advertising, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention. UMP requests privacy information and manages consent or opt-out choices using device and app information, regional signals and your selections; ad requests are made only when UMP reports that they may be. Where available, use Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices to review or change your choices, and Android's ad settings to reset or delete your advertising ID. Local tools keep working if ads cannot load.

Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy · How Google uses information from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

We do not run a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Sharing

We do not sell documents or any personal information, and we do not use document content for advertising. Ad-related information may be shared with Google and eligible ad partners as described above when ads are enabled; some privacy laws describe certain advertising disclosures as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — use the ad privacy choices to exercise available opt-outs.

Files leave the app's private storage only when you save or share them through another app, folder or provider, whose privacy rules then apply. Google Play handles downloads, any payments and reviews under its own terms; ItziPDF has no subscriptions or in-app purchases in this release.

Support and external links

If you contact support, we use your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send to answer your request. Please do not send confidential documents unless they are needed for your request; feedback never attaches a document automatically. We keep support correspondence only as long as needed for the request and legitimate record-keeping or legal obligations. Opening our website or social links takes you to services with their own privacy practices.

Security and retention

ItziPDF uses app-private storage, Android's selected-file access, and on-device processing. Password-protected PDFs you create use strong encryption (AES-256 by default); keep your passwords safe, because they cannot be recovered. No storage or network system can guarantee absolute security.

Library files, saved signatures, recipes and other local records remain until you delete them, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Clearing data or uninstalling removes them; Android may remove cache files at any time. Copies saved outside the private Library are independent and are not deleted with it. Google and other external providers apply their own retention and deletion policies to data they process.

Your choices and rights

You decide which files to open, can delete Library files and saved signatures, manage folder access, clear the thumbnail cache, change advertising choices where available, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Contact itzigroup_support@itzigroup.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of support information held by Itzi Group. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to object, restrict processing, data portability, or to complain to your local privacy authority. We may need to verify a request and may keep information where the law requires. Requests about Google's information can be made through Google's privacy controls.

Children

ItziPDF is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes; the effective date identifies the version. We keep the policy in the app and on our website aligned with the released app.
